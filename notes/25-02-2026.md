## Further Image Quality Analysis Avenues

Now that we can match SPP output closely (RMSE ~13.45), the question becomes: **can we measure and potentially improve beyond SPP?**

### A. Noise Analysis

**What to measure:**
- Noise variance at different luminance levels (shadows vs. midtones vs. highlights)
- ISO-dependent noise characteristics
- Compare noise power spectrum (frequency domain)

**How to measure:**
- Find flat gray or low-detail areas in images
- Calculate standard deviation of pixel values in uniform regions
- For each luminance bin (0-25, 25-50, etc.), compute noise statistics

**Potential improvement:**
- If our noise is higher than SPP in shadows, we could add smarter denoising
- If our noise is lower (less smoothing), we preserve more detail

### B. Dynamic Range / Highlight Headroom

**What to measure:**
- Percentage of pixels that are fully clipped (R=G=B=255 in 8-bit)
- Percentage recoverable (some channels clipped, others not)
- Histogram analysis at high end

**How to measure:**
- Count pixels at 255 for each channel
- Analyze channel-by-channel clipping patterns
- Compare with Foveon's per-pixel RGB advantage for reconstruction

**Potential improvement:**
- Foveon has advantage: if R is clipped but G/B are not, can estimate R from G/B ratio
- This is what the channel reconstruction function was attempting

### C. Shadow Detail Preservation

**What to measure:**
- SNR at different shadow levels
- Whether shadows are being "lifted" (losing contrast) vs. properly rendered
- Compare noise floor between our output and SPP

**How to measure:**
- Analyze histogram in dark regions
- Measure local contrast in shadows
- Compare shadow noise patterns

### D. Color Accuracy Beyond RMSE

**What to measure:**
- Convert RGB to CIE Lab color space
- Measure Delta E (perceptual color difference)
- Analyze hue shifts in specific color ranges

**Potential improvement:**
- RMSE treats all errors equally, but Delta E better matches human perception
- Could reveal color shifts we're missing

### E. Sharpness / MTF Analysis

**What to measure:**
- Edge response (10-90% rise distance)
- Compare unsharpened output vs. SPP's aggressive sharpening

**Potential improvement:**
- SPP cannot disable sharpening - we could offer sharper output
- Or match SPP's sharpening more precisely
