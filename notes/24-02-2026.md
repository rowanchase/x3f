2. **Threshold 0.80** (clipped = >= 0.80):

3. **Disabled old highlight desaturation** (at 0.6 threshold):
   - Result: RMSE jumped to 35.2!
   - Analysis: Old highlight desaturation is ESSENTIAL

### Key Findings

- The old highlight desaturation (lines 967-985 in x3f_process.c) runs AFTER color conversion
- It uses a 0.6 threshold and desaturates towards white
- This is doing the heavy lifting for highlight handling
- My channel reconstruction at raw RGB level is redundant/overlapping

- The CAMF parameters (0.75, 1.5) don't match the normalized data range
- Need to understand what space these parameters are in
