### R/B Correction Experiments

Tested various R/B boost values (1.03, 1.04, 1.05, 1.08):

**Finding**: R/B boost helps some files but makes others worse. The effect is highly image-dependent.

### Root Cause Analysis

The R/B underexposure appears to be **image-dependent**, not systematic:
- Some images (0993, 0927, 0991) have significant R/B underexposure
- Other images (0994, 0990) already have balanced or over-exposed R/B channels

This variation is likely due to:
1. Different shooting conditions (lighting: daylight, indoor, flash)
2. Different white balance processing in SPP per image
3. Scene-dependent color rendering
