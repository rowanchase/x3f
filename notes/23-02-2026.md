## ISO Analysis

Discovered files have varying ISO:
- ISO 200 (ratio 2.0): Most files
- ISO 400 (ratio 4.0): Files 1003, 1008

### Failed Experiment: ISO-relative exposure compensation

Tried: `exposure_comp = 2.6 * (200 / capture_iso)`
- For ISO 200: 2.6x (unchanged)
- For ISO 400: 1.3x (reduced)

Result: Made ISO 400 files worse (RMSE increased from 30 to 37-40)
Conclusion: SPP does NOT use ISO-relative exposure compensation. The 2.6x works across all ISOs.
